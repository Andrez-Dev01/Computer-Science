# PostgreSQL Arrays

## What is it?

> [!abstract] Quick meaning
> PostgreSQL can store arrays as a database value and query or update their elements.
^what-is-it

## Why it matters

This is conceptually related to collections, but PostgreSQL arrays are not raw contiguous CPU arrays exposed to your program.
