## Tiny rule

**Chapter = where I learned it.**  
**Concept = what it means.**

## Hover-preview link

In a chapter note:

`[[Assembly Language#^what-is-it|Assembly Language]]`

The concept page contains:

```md
## What is it?

> [!abstract] Quick meaning
> Short explanation here.
^what-is-it
```

Hover the chapter link to preview that block.

## Reuse

If `Registers` appears in Chapters 2, 3, 4, 5, and 8, all of them link to the **same** `Registers.md`.

Do not make `Registers Chapter 2.md`, `Registers Chapter 5.md`, etc.
