**Every subject uses the same three areas:** `01 - College Courses`, `02 - Independent Learning`, and `03 - Quick Reference`. Everything required to learn a topic can stay **inside the relevant section/lesson note**. Quick references are optional, on-demand summaries, not a mandatory duplicate concept library.

**Actual application project notes belong in the other vault** (`Software-Projects-Vault`).
